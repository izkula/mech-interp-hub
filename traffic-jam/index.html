SNARL

Exit here for gridlock

It's 8:00 AM in Portside and everyone west of the river is trying to get downtown. You get one fender-bender. Put it where it hurts most.

How to play

  • Scout. Watch the traffic. Pinch, double-tap or use + and − to zoom; drag to pan. Red tail lights mean braking.
  • Pick a lane. Click any lane to see its flow and speed, then press Crash here.
  • Watch it spread. The crash blocks that lane until the tow truck clears it after 5 minutes. Scoring runs 8 minutes.

Your score is the extra delay your crash causes, in vehicle-minutes. A copy of the same morning runs alongside with no crash, so only the delay you caused counts.

HOW IT WORKS

Every car is simulated individually, with the models traffic engineers use.

Driving

  • Car following uses the Intelligent Driver Model. Each driver has their own reaction gap, acceleration and preferred speed. Trucks and buses are slower to get going.
  • Lane changes follow MOBIL. A driver moves over when the other lane is faster and the car behind can brake safely. Drivers also get into the right lane before a turn or exit.
  • Rubbernecking. Drivers slow to look at the crash, in both directions.

The city

  • Traffic lights run fixed two-phase cycles, timed as a green wave along Main St.
  • Don't block the box. Drivers won't enter an intersection they can't clear. When a queue backs up to the corner, cross traffic still flows, but that approach stalls on green.
  • Freeway on-ramps merge by yielding to gaps. An off-ramp that backs up spills onto the freeway.

Routing

  • 40% of drivers use a navigation app. It re-plans their route every few seconds from live speeds and knows about your crash. Everyone else takes their usual route.

Scoring

Every driver's lost time is added up: each minute spent crawling or stopped when they'd normally be moving. The game runs the exact same morning twice, once with your crash and once without, and your score is the difference in vehicle-minutes. That's why queues at ordinary red lights don't count.

The round keeps going after the tow truck because the queue takes a while to drain, and drivers are still losing time. Once the lane reopens the game fast-forwards and ends as soon as the jam has cleared (at most 8 minutes after the crash).
